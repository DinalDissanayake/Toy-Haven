A Note from the Shop Floor

Thirty-nine years of shelves, stories, and the thrill of the hunt.

We opened Toy Haven in 1987 with a single wooden counter in Kurunegala and two dozen diecast cars. While decades have passed, that electric feeling of finding the exact piece you've been looking for hasn't changed at all.

Whether you're hunting down an elusive fighting-game figure, picking up a wooden railway for your child's birthday, or adding a 1:64 racer to your work desk, we treat every box like it's going onto our own family display shelf.

— Nimal & Kasun Perera Founders & Curators, Toy Haven
